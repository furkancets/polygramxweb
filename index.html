Security & Privacy

Enterprise-Grade Privacy by Design

We understand that engineering knowledge is sensitive. PolygramX is built from the ground up with data privacy and compliance at its core.

🔐

Anonymized by Default

Personal identifiers are stripped before anything enters the knowledge base. No engineer is personally linked to any contribution.

🛡️

GDPR & KVKK Compliant

Designed for European data protection standards. Full compliance with both GDPR and Turkish KVKK regulations out of the box.

🏢

Your Data Stays Yours

Knowledge bases are completely isolated per organization. Your company's intelligence is never shared with or accessible to other companies.

🔑

Hashed & Encrypted

All contributions are cryptographically hashed. Sensitive data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard protocols.
